I'm an author, AI Craft leader, and hands-on AI workflow builder from New
Zealand. I help non-coders direct serious builds and help technical builders
make AI-assisted systems more reliable, reviewable, and useful. Most examples
use synthetic data, make no model call, and keep consequential decisions with
people.

## Featured Tools

- [EvidenceGate](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/evidencegate) leaves a
  revision-bound receipt for human-reviewed AI-assisted work.
- [Context Boundary Examples](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/context-boundary-examples)
  checks whether answers stay inside supplied evidence, while
  [Agent Action Authority Examples](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/agent-action-authority-examples)
  checks whether an approval still matches the proposed action.
- [Context Contract Compiler](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/context-contract-compiler)
  protects required context, explains selection decisions, and rejects stale
  receipts after declared material changes.
- [Public Repo Safety Kit](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/public-repo-safety-kit)
  checks public-candidate repositories for common privacy, secret, path, and
  commit-identity risks before publication.

The [Local Assistant Reliability Lab](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/local-assistant-reliability-lab)
is the front door to every stable guide, tool, and pattern. Agents and tools
can read the same catalog from its
[machine-readable toolkit index](https://github.com/TheDarkniteFalls/local-assistant-reliability-lab/blob/main/toolkit_index.json).
